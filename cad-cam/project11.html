Sketch2Solid

← All projects

Project 11 turns three 2D orthographic sketches into a 3D solid. Upload or load sample top, front, and side views, then the app segments each image, intersects the silhouettes in voxel space, smooths the result, and runs marching cubes to produce a watertight mesh you can inspect in 3D and export as STL.

Author: ME23B042 PRANESH VIJAYAKUMAR Original: Python / tkinter + matplotlib desktop app
