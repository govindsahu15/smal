Project 9 is a 2D curve design and visualization tool for CAD/CAM workflows. It lets you build and compare Bezier, B-spline, and NURBS curves from the same control points — adjust degree, knot spacing, and per-point weights, then export the final path as CSV coordinates or G-code for machining.
